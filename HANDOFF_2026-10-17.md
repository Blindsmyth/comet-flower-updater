## Rig plan (owner decision)
Step 1: 5-slot CF patch (comp -> Muff -> CF Watt -> Mistress/CE-2 -> delay), native per-slot on/off and pages.
Step 2: CF W/D/W mixer in the last slot producing the **stereo out** (dry centre from an amp-slot publisher via a
shared buffer in free SDRAM c0187024..c01f4000, wet L/R from 100 %-wet time effects). USB taps must never
compromise the input/output audio (read-only, bounded, off unless USB mode).
