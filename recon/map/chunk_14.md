## Open questions

- c015a520/c015a7a0 (shared_dispatch_core_a/b, 640B each) are called by nearly every task wrapper in the firmware but were only partially traced; a dedicated pass walking the c01819c8/cc/d0/d4 table chain and the 0xc016ba40/0xc016de00 fixed handlers would likely resolve what the generic event/message dispatch mechanism actually is.
