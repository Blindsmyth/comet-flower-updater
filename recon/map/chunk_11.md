## Open questions
- **Facts-sheet boundary gaps.** At least four function starts are missing from `build/fw/map/facts/chunk_11.md`'s address list and were discovered only by reading the disassembly byte-for-byte: c0149e80, c0149ef8 (both filled in above, between the original c0149de4 and c014a010 rows), c014c66c (lumped into c014c650's reported size), and c014c3c0/c014c3ce (both lumped into c014c3b4's reported size, which really covers three unrelated tiny functions separated by explicit returns). Any other helper who re-ran `facts.py` on this chunk should check whether this is a one-off gap or a systemic issue with how function boundaries are enumerated, especially around short functions preceded by alignment NOPs.
- **c014a58c (usb_interface_activate_core, 1396 bytes)** -- only its first ~90 of ~349 instructions were read. Its early descriptor-register step and the DINT critical section with the c0180000+0x19bc/0x1ae4/0x1ae8/0x1f74 globals need a dedicated pass if the USB stack is ever in scope.
- **c014b06c / c014b300 / c014b37c (tuner + numeric-display formatters, 660/124/1668 bytes)** -- only skimmed; the exact quantity c014b06c formats (time? frequency? a calibration percentage?) and the bulk of c014b37c's pitch-to-frequency math were not traced.
- **c014bee0's full-arena memset reached from mainapp_frame_update** -- is this really unconditional on every frame, or is the c014bf20 call path from mainapp_frame_update gated by something upstream that only fires rarely (first frame, patch reload)? A 409600-byte clear every frame would be a lot of cycles.
- **c0140630 (fact-sheet alias "midi_tx_if_whitelisted")** is used by c014c44c/c014c5b0 purely to set an edited character and mark it changed, with no MIDI involved -- its existing name may be too narrow for what it actually does; worth reconciling with whichever chunk first named it.
- **field-type dispatch (c014c66c)** only confirmed types 25/26/27 map to CHAR/LEVEL/SKIP by table-slot order; the 4th slot's field-type value (presumably 28, "VALUE") and its own dispatcher (if any, distinct from c014c66c) were not located.
- **c014c880 / c014c9f0 / c014ccd0 (patchlist file-scan family, 368/736/112 bytes)** -- only the opening of c014c880 was read; c014c9f0 and c014ccd0 were named from their shared call list and strings alone, not independently traced. If patch/preset save-load or file-browser work is ever in scope, this family (plus the c0100000-based name tables in Data) needs a dedicated pass.
- **c014cef4 (mainapp_popup_dispatch, 456 bytes)** -- named and roled from its call list only (calibration popup, update-complete/error popups, calibration helpers); not traced instruction-by-instruction. Its dispatch condition (which popup fires when) is unknown.
- **c014c758's apparent self-call** at c014c806 (`CALLP 0xc014c758,B3` with args 98,6) was read directly in the disassembly and is not an artifact of a boundary-gap miscount, but its purpose (recursion? drawing a second instance?) is unexplained.
