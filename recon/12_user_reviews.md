# 12 — What G1 Four / G1X Four reviews complain about

Date: 2026-10-06, quick web search, to steer which effects are worth
improving first.

- **Drive and distortion are the weak spot.** Reviewers call the
  distortion harsh ("the worst distortion I've ever heard"), "90s digital"
  and "stiff"; high gain is singled out as weaker than costlier units. That
  fits recon/11: a shared 4x IIR with only -60 dB at ~45 kHz (aliasing) and
  formula clippers instead of circuit models.
- **Noise:** some drives and amps are noisy; people add the downloadable
  noise gate.
- **Good:** clean and moderate-gain amps, delays, reverbs and modulation.
- **Limits people mention:** 5 effects per chain; a learning curve.

So drives and high-gain amps are where CF work is most visible to users;
time-based effects are less urgent.
