## 4. CF Master Architectural Assessment (For Argon / Future Synthesis)

When compiling `cfmaster` (monolithic 5-slot) or its split variants (`cfm_pre` and `cfm_amp`), the TI C6000 compiler produces code sizes exceeding the 40 KiB slot limit because each stage incorporates high-order oversampled non-linear models and full nodal matrix derivations:
1. `hiwatt_amp.h` (~24.5 KiB .text alone) + `ra200_g1.h` (~22.8 KiB .text alone) = 47.3 KiB of code, even before Big Muff, Mistress, or Delay are linked in.
2. `cfm_pre` (DynaComp + Muff + Mistress) compiles to 29.4 KiB `.text` + 11.6 KiB `.const` (muff_blob) = 42,032 bytes total slot image (exceeds 40,960 B by 1,072 B).
3. **Hardware Decision Gate**: The multi-slot probes `CFM2P` and `CFM5P` in Updater 24 will measure whether Zoom's runtime loader allocates an expanded contiguous heap for multi-slot effects (`N × 40 KiB`).
   - If **YES** (e.g. 5-slot effect receives 200 KiB heap), `cfmaster.ZD2` can load directly by adjusting the retarget script's slot limit check.
   - If **NO** (loader enforces 40 KiB per effect regardless of `g1_slots`), Argon will be engaged to perform code deduplication (e.g. sharing the half-band polyphase IIR downsamplers across stages, sharing the tanh tables, or using compact straight-line coefficient generators).
