## Decisions this session
- **CF Watt runs 2x** (owner): blind ABX on clean takes showed the 2x aliasing inaudible; `hiwatt.c` defaults
  `HW_OS 2` (`#ifndef`, host tools build 4x with /DHW_OS=4). Coefficients cached in the slot work buffer, keyed by
  the smoothed knobs **and** HW_FS_OS (bit-identical, 32-bit host test).
- Lesson: the "2x noise burst" was **clipping in the USB tap** (ch 3-4 pre-master float -> 16 bit saturates);
  record A/B with headroom (patch level 80). `ua_ab compare` and CF Monitor warn on full scale.
- Codex/Sol gone for good; Argon (agy) quota until ~2026-10-23; Sonnet subagents for helper work.
